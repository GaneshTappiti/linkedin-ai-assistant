## 🪝 Hook Formula (3 Parts, <25 Words)

```
[TOPIC SIGNAL] + [COUNTERINTUITIVE FACT] + [PROMISE]

Examples:

"Most founders validate wrong. That's why 90% fail." ✅
(Topic: Founder validation | Surprise | Implied lesson)

"We thought X was important. Users wanted Y." ✅
(Topic: MVP validation | Learning | Real experience)

"Design-minded engineers outship everyone (5x). Here's why." ✅
(Topic: Design-engineering | Data | Promise of insight)
```

**🚫 Don't do this:**
- "Check this out 👀" ❌ (No topic signal)
- "10 tips for success" ❌ (Generic, not framework)
- "You won't believe..." ❌ (Engagement bait, penalized)

## ✅ Pre-Launch Checklist (Before Posting)

```
Topic Check:
[ ] Clearly in one of my 3 topics (Founder, AI/Ed, Design)
[ ] Keywords present (validation, EdTech, design, engineering, etc.)

Hook Check:
[ ] First 1-2 sentences are punchy (<20 words)
[ ] Contains data, counterintuition, or specific surprise
[ ] Makes someone want to read "why"

Content Check:
[ ] Format is Tier 1 or 2 (carousel, framework, long-form, case study)
[ ] Uses MY real projects (Scollab, Buildtrix, gift biz) + metrics
[ ] Has an actionable framework or system (not just story)
[ ] Human-written, not AI-generated feel

Save Signals:
[ ] Contains a lesson/framework worth having forever
[ ] Provides specific guidance (not generic tips)
[ ] Has visual elements (carousel, design, screenshot)

CTA Check:
[ ] Ends with genuine question (not rhetorical)
[ ] Invites audience to share experiences
[ ] Natural, not "like this post" 🚫
```

## 💡 Reverse-Engineering Template

**Find a high-performing post in your niche (~50K+ saves vs average)**

1. **Analyze Hook**
   - First 1-2 sentences
   - Topic signal? Surprise element?
   - Why did it stop scrolling?

2. **Note Format**
   - Carousel, long-form, infographic?
   - Structure/flow?
   - Visual elements?

3. **Extract Principle**
   - Why did 360 Brew amplify this?
   - What framework/system is it teaching?

4. **Adapt for You**
   - Same hook structure, YOUR story
   - Your projects, their format
   - Your audience, their principle

**Example:**
- Found: EdTech carousel "3 mistakes in building campus tools"
- Hook: "Campus software fails because..." (specific problem)
- Format: Carousel (8 slides, visual + text per slide)
- Your version: "3 mistakes building Scollab (and how we fixed them)"
