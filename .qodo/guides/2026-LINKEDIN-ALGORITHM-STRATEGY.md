## 🔥 The Game Changed (360 Brew Algorithm)

LinkedIn replaced its old engagement-based ranking with **360 Brew**, an AI model that prioritizes **semantic relevance** over raw metrics.

### Old System vs New System
| Metric | Old LinkedIn | 360 Brew (2026) |
|--------|------------|-----------------|
| Primary Signal | Likes, Comments, Shares | Saves, Semantic Relevance |
| Content Type Preference | Engagement bait | Educational frameworks |
| Hook Importance | Normal | 3-5x more valuable |
| Format Preference | Mixed | Carousels/Infographics #1 |
| Topic Consistency | Optional | 80% in 3 topics = huge boost |
| Depth Requirement | Short is fine | Long-form, deep > short |
| AI Content | Tolerated | Penalized |
| Validation | Engagement | Saves + Dwelling Time |

## 📊 Content Planning Template (For You)

### Before Writing Any Post:

**Topic Check:**
- [ ] Post is clearly in one of my 3 core topics
- [ ] Algorithm will recognize the domain (has keywords)
- [ ] OR it's inspirational/discipline content (20% allowance)

**Hook Check:**
- [ ] First 1-2 sentences are strong (counterintuitive, data-driven, or curiosity-based)
- [ ] Hook establishes topic relevance immediately
- [ ] Hook makes someone want to read "why"

**Format Check:**
- [ ] Format choice is Tier 1/2 (not low-performing Tier 3)
- [ ] Content will have dwelling time (carousel, visuals, length)
- [ ] Framework/system/step-by-step structure included

**Authenticity Check:**
- [ ] Uses YOUR real projects (Scollab, Buildtrix, gift business)
- [ ] Includes YOUR data/metrics (not generic advice)
- [ ] Written in YOUR voice (technical, honest, founder energy)
- [ ] If story: Includes actionable lesson, not just emotion

**CTA Check:**
- [ ] CTA is genuine question (not rhetorical)
- [ ] Question invites real answers from audience
- [ ] Goal: Comments with substance, not generic replies

### Example: Does This Post Work?

**Draft Post:**
"Most founders fail because they don't validate. Here's how to do it right.

At Buildtrix, we use the Mom Test method...
[explains framework]

Try this this week.
Questions?"

**Analysis:**
- ❌ Topic Check: Generic advice, not clearly YOUR story
- ❌ Hook Check: Generic opening, no data, no curiosity
- ❌ Format Check: Text only, will get low dwelling time
- ❌ Authenticity Check: Could be AI-generated content
- ❌ CTA Check: "Questions?" is rhetorical/lazy

**Improved Version:**
"Week 2 Mom Test results. Everything we assumed about founder validation was wrong.

Here's what 50 founders actually told us vs what we expected:
[carousel with 3-4 real examples from Buildtrix]

[Framework slide]: The pattern that predicted success
[Lesson slide]: Why we're changing our roadmap

What was your biggest validation surprise?"

**Analysis:**
- ✅ Topic Check: Founder validation + Buildtrix = clear topic match
- ✅ Hook Check: Real data + surprise signal = curiosity hook
- ✅ Format Check: Carousel = high dwelling time, easy to visualize
- ✅ Authenticity Check: YOUR real data from Buildtrix Week 2
- ✅ CTA Check: Real question, invites founder stories

## 🚀 Reverse-Engineering Competitors (High-Leverage)

### The 5-10x Rule (Ganesh's Hack)
Find posts in your niche that got 5-10x more engagement than usual. These are signals of what 360 Brew rewards.

**Process:**

1. **Identify Outliers**
   - Check EdTech accounts, Buildtrix-like tools, design-engineering builders
   - Look for posts with much higher saves/comments than their average
   - Copy the URL

2. **Analyze the Framework**
   - Hook: What makes someone stop scrolling?
   - Structure: Is it carousel, text, framework, story?
   - Data: Do they share numbers/proof?
   - Length: Is it long-form or concise?

3. **Extract the Principle**
   - What pattern made this work?
   - Why did 360 Brew amplify it?
   - Does it align with algorithm signals (saves, topic, depth)?

4. **Adapt for You**
   - Same hook structure, different content
   - Your story, their framework
   - Your projects, their principle

**Example:**
```
Found: EdTech founder posted carousel about "3 validation mistakes EdTechs make"
- Got 2K saves (10x her average)
- Format: Carousel (8 slides)
- Hook: Specific problem + preview of solutions

Adapted for Ganesh:
"3 validation mistakes we made building Scollab (don't repeat them)"
- Carousel format with Scollab examples
- Same hook structure ("mistakes + teaching")
- Your data, similar framework
- Post to Scollab audience (EdTech builders)
```

## 📈 Metrics That Matter (360 Brew + Long-Term)

**360 Brew Signals:**
- 📍 Saves (3-5x more important than likes)
- 📍 Dwelling time (carousel, long content)
- 📍 Topic consistency (80% in 3 topics)
- 📍 Human authenticity (original voice, your work)
- 📍 Educational value (frameworks, systems, lessons)

**Long-Term Signals (What YOU Should Track):**
- Quality of comments (substance > emoji reacts)
- Collaboration offers (DMs from interesting people)
- Audience growth in target persona (student founders, EdTech builders)
- Content distribution across posts (sees patterns)
- Post-to-action conversion (posts → Buildtrix signups, Scollab interest)

**Don't Track:**
- ❌ Total followers (vanity metric)
- ❌ Likes (360 Brew deprioritizes)
- ❌ Views (irrelevant if not saving/engaging)
- ❌ Click-throughs without context
