## My positions

1. **WhatsApp groups are killing campus culture:** They leak personal numbers to strangers, breed spam, and vanish on graduation day. Campus life needs a real, verified student OS.
2. **The .edu email assumption is an American myth that excludes 2M+ Indian students:** In India, 78%+ of private college students use personal Gmail. You must solve trust through database registries, not admin paperwork.
3. **Placement cells create toxic competition:** Students are told to hoard notes and hide project ideas from classmates. Cross-campus guilds (collaborating across 15+ colleges in a city) are 10x more valuable than placement prep alone.
4. **Student fests fail on unit economics, not enthusiasm:** Paying ₹349 proves students will buy tickets, but relying on ticket sales alone to cover venue deposits is a trap. You have to lock in brand sponsors upfront.
