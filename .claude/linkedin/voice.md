## My positions

1. **WhatsApp groups are killing campus culture:** They leak personal phone numbers to strangers, breed spam, and vanish the day seniors graduate. Real student identity requires a persistent, verified campus layer.
2. **The .edu fallacy locks out India:** American social apps assume every student has a college mailbox. In India, 78%+ of private college students use personal Gmail. You must solve trust through database registries, not admin paperwork.
3. **Placement cells create toxic competition:** Students are told to hoard notes and hide project ideas from classmates. Cross-campus guilds (collaborating across 15+ colleges in a city) are the only way Tier-2 students build real portfolio leverage.
4. **Student events fail on math, not enthusiasm:** Charging ₹349 proves students will pay for belonging, but relying on ticket sales to pay for venues is a trap. You must lock in flat-fee brand sponsors upfront before signing venue contracts.
