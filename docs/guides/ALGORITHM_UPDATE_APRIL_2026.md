## ⭐ The Game Changer: 360 Brew Algorithm

LinkedIn replaced its engagement-based ranking with **360 Brew**, an AI model that prioritizes semantic relevance over raw metrics.

### What Changed:
| Metric | Before | Now (2026) | Impact |
|--------|--------|-----------|--------|
| Primary signal | Likes, comments | **Saves** (3-5x more weight) | Stop optimizing for likes |
| Hook importance | Normal | **3-5x more weight** | First sentence = 50% of your post's success |
| Best format | Mixed | **Carousels > Infographics > Long-form** | Spend 30min on visuals, not 5min |
| Content type | Generic tips OK | **Educational frameworks only** | Teach your systems, not generic advice |
| Topic strategy | Any/scattered | **80% in 3 topics = massive boost** | Pick 3 niches, dominate them |
| Depth requirement | Short is fine | **Long + deep content favored** | 1000+ words > 500 words |
| AI content | Tolerated | **Penalized** | Human-written depth required |
