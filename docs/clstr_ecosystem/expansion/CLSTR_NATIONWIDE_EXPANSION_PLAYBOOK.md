## 1. The Short Version
Clstr is a verified network for college students in India. It starts by filling one city completely, Visakhapatnam (Vizag), then repeats the same process city by city until it covers the country. 

The strategy is **depth before breadth**. A city where every nearby college is on Clstr is worth more than thousands of scattered signups across India, because students only stay if they see people they know and people they want to meet.

Two lines sum up the pitch:
* **“Your college gets you a degree. Clstr gets you a city.”**
* **“Find your people. Vizag is the campus.”**

Vizag is one phase. It is the proof, not the destination. The plan is every city, then all of India.

---

## 2. The Problem Clstr Solves
Students in India are mostly stuck inside their own campus. The usual way to reach students from other colleges is a WhatsApp group, and those groups have four problems: anyone can join, phone numbers get exposed to strangers, spam takes over, and the group dies when the senior admins graduate. Groups also tend to fall apart once they pass about 200 members.

The other options do not fit either:
* **LinkedIn** is too broad and too professional for a first-year student.
* **Internshala and Unstop** are about careers and nothing else.
* **Instagram** connects you to influencers you will never meet. Clstr connects you to the developer in the next building who can help you win a hackathon this Saturday.
* Most global platforms assume every student has an official college email. The top institutes (IITs, NITs, BITS) do. Clstr’s research shows over **78% of private colleges in India do not give students email accounts**, relying on a single admin Gmail instead. A platform that needs a college email shuts out most of the market.

## 3. What Clstr Is
Clstr is a family of products under one unified brand:
1. **`clstr.in`** is the main social OS. It provides verified campus feeds, messaging, hackathon/startup team recruitment (CollabHub), verified club pages, event discovery, an EcoCampus peer marketplace, mock interview bookings with placed seniors, verified job listings, and personal portfolio links.
2. **`vizag.clstr.in`** is the offline student community for Visakhapatnam. It runs events, interest groups (Circles), and builder guilds (Guilds) so online connections turn into real friendships.
3. **`spark.clstr.in`** handles event registration, dynamic rolling-QR tickets, offline gate check-ins, and automated certificate generation for student clubs and fests.

---

## 4. How Trust Works
Every person on Clstr is a verified student:
1. **Students with a college email** verify via email OTP. Clstr resolves known email aliases (e.g. `@au.edu.in` and `@andhrauniversity.ac.in`).
2. **Students without official college email** sign up with personal email and phone OTP, then select their college from the audited `no_edu_colleges` registry covering 36 Indian states and union territories.
3. **Spectator Mode:** While a student waits for email provisioning, they can browse feeds, explore teams, and RSVP for events, but cannot post, comment, message, or sell. Enforced strictly at the database level.

**Student Trust Covenant:** Clstr never sells student contact lists or phone numbers to telemarketers or coaching institutes. Phone numbers are never exposed to other students.

Vizag is the test bed for all ground doctrines:
* **The 4 Clusters (19 Colleges):**
  * *North:* GVPCE, Raghu Engineering College, Raghu IT, ANITS, Chaitanya EC, Sanketika, BABA, Avanthi Tagarapuvalasa.
  * *City Center:* Andhra University CE (AUCE), Dr. L. Bullayya College, GVP Degree, GITAM Deemed.
  * *South:* VIIT Duvvada, Vignan's VIEW Women's College.
  * *West (Anakapalli):* Dadi Institute (DIET), Avanthi Makavarapalem, Sai Ganapathi, Viswanadha, Al-Aman.
* **Curfew Doctrine:** Over 65% of female hostel students in Vizag have 5:30 PM to 7:00 PM curfews. Public community events run **Sundays between 1:30 PM and 5:30 PM**, and never past 6:00 PM.
* **Neutral Venue Rotation:** Events rotate across neutral venues (Dr. L. Bullayya College, A-Hub, public beaches) so Clstr is never seen as a single college's project.
* **Guilds & Circles:**
  * *Guilds (Careers & Building):* Code & AI Guild, Design & UI/UX Guild, Product & Founder Guild, Creator Guild.
  * *Circles (Culture & Belonging):* Sunset Beach Socials, Music & Jamming, Sports & Box Turf, Book & Philosophy.

## 7. Gated Expansion Rules (Gates Instead of Dates)
* **Gate 1 (Opens Vijayawada Pilot):**
  * At least 5 of 15 core Vizag campuses meet the 5-Point Activation Floor for two consecutive 6-week cycles.
  * $\ge 30\%$ repeat attendance across 3 consecutive Spark events.
  * 2 paying brand sponsors locked in, 1 renewed.
  * Corporate incorporation (Pvt Ltd / LLP) complete and security test delivered.
  * One Sunday event delivered without the founder needing to be on-site.
* **Gate 2 (Opens Hyderabad Outer Belt):**
  * At least half the target colleges in Vijayawada and Guntur meet the activation floor, and one Sunday event runs there autonomously.
* **Gate 3 (Each Subsequent City):**
  * The preceding city passes its density and autonomy tests before launching the next.
