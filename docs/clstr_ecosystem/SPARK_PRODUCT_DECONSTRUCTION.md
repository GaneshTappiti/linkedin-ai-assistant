---

## 🔑 The Three Jobs Nobody Wants to Do (Solved)

| Job # | Feature | How Spark Does It |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **01** | **Sign-ups** | Shareable event page with poster, price, and details. Attendees pay directly to the club's own UPI. The club approves in 1 tap. |
| **02** | **The Gate** | Every approved attendee gets a ticket code & phone QR pass. Volunteers check people in from any phone with a **<400ms scan time**. Watch attendance live. |
| **03** | **Certificates** | Upload template, click where the name goes, hit generate. **600 personalised certificates** with zero spreadsheet work. Files auto-purge after 15 days to save storage and ensure privacy. |

## 💰 The Non-Custodial Money Promise

> **"WE NEVER TOUCH YOUR MONEY."**  
> *Spark does not collect, hold or process payments. For a paid event you put your own UPI ID or payment link on the page. Attendees pay you. You see it land in your app, and you approve the registration. No gateway fees. No settlement wait. No cut for us. Ever.*

## ⚔️ The Competitive Moat: Google Forms vs. Townscript vs. Spark

| Dimension | Google Forms + Sheets | Townscript / Commercial Gateways | Spark by Clstr |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Platform Fee** | 0% (Free) | 4% – 8% + 18% GST | **0% (Always Free for Clubs)** |
| **Payment Flow** | Manual UPI screenshot upload | Gateway (Requires business PAN/KYC) | **Direct Club UPI (Peer-to-Peer)** |
| **Gate Entry Passes** | None (Volunteers scroll sheets) | Standard PDF tickets | **Phone QR Passes (<400ms scan)** |
| **Automated Certificates** | Manual Canva mail merge (Hours) | Paid add-on / None | **1-Click Bulk PDF (15d auto-purge)** |
| **Settlement Speed** | Instant in personal account | 3 to 7 business days | **Instant (Lands directly in club UPI)** |
| **Capacity Control** | None (Frequent overbooking) | Hard seat limits | **Automated seat capping & waitlists** |
