# Clstr Vizag Community — Investment Committee Killer Questions

**Scope:** vizag.clstr.in as a student community; clstr.in is treated as the supporting technology layer, not the entire investment thesis.

**Reference date:** 23 August 2026

## The investment question

Can Clstr become a durable, trusted, cross-college student community in Vizag that repeatedly creates student value and captures enough sponsor, partner, institutional or event revenue to support a real operating company?

The committee will not underwrite “large student population,” “many event possibilities,” “campus ambassadors” or “companies will want exposure” without evidence. It will underwrite repeated behavior, trusted reach, event economics, partner renewals and operating repeatability.

## Minimum evidence before a serious investor meeting

The community must produce a verified institution-and-student map, active-node dashboard, retained student cohorts, event P&Ls, one paid or contractually secured sponsor pilot, post-event partner report, product usage between events, safety and privacy controls, clean entity/IP records, and a 12-month cash plan.

Without this package, the investor is not evaluating a community business. The investor is evaluating conviction.
