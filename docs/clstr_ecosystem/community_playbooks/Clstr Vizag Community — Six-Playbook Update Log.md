## Canonical rules now applied

### Community versus technology

Every community document must say that **Clstr is the community** and **clstr.in is the supporting technology layer** for identity, discovery, registration, continuity, event history, connections and measurement.

### Category claim

Use **cross-college student social-discovery community for Vizag**. Do not claim that no student community exists. Do not make an absolute “first” or “only” claim without a narrow, documented category and evidence.

### Market claims

The 131-institution figure, 100,000 engineering-student figure and 200,000–300,000 total-student figure are not treated as verified facts. The 19-college list is an initial target list, not confirmed coverage. Build an institution-level database before publishing a market denominator.

### Event claims

The 12-month calendar is a target sequence, not a hard promise. Event capacity is subject to venue, permissions, safety, age eligibility, safeguarding, funding, staffing, consent, registration and follow-up.

### Revenue claims

No sponsor revenue is treated as forecast or committed until a written agreement exists and cash is collected. Pricing ranges are negotiation anchors only and should not appear as validated willingness-to-pay. In-kind value is reported separately from cash revenue.

### Compensation claims

No fixed salary, stipend, bonus or guaranteed revenue share is promised while the community is unfunded. Volunteer recognition and leadership access must not be described as employment or as a substitute for cash compensation.

### KPI claims

Raw signups, follower counts, QR scans, ambassador count and event count are secondary. The primary evidence is verified meaningful participation, repeat activity, repeat attendance, cross-college connections, completed collaborations, event contribution margin, sponsor renewal, safety and founder independence.

### Data and safety

No general student contact list is sold or included in sponsorship. Direct student contact or opportunity flows require a specific purpose, clear explanation and separate opt-in. Minor participation, photography, moderation and physical-event safety require explicit operating controls.
