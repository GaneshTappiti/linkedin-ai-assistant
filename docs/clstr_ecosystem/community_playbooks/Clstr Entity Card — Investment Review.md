## Entity-resolution conclusion

The investment review can assess the business thesis and diligence requirements, but it cannot make a financing decision without the legal entity, cap table, historical financials, product analytics, customer/sponsor evidence, contracts, security posture and founder references. Any valuation, ownership or cheque recommendation would be premature without those materials.
