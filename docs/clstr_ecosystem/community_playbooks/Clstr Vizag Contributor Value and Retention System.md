# Clstr Vizag Contributor Value and Retention System

## Why should anyone join, stay and work?

**Clstr cannot retain people with ambition alone.** A student will stay only when the work gives them a return that is visible, credible and worth more than the time and effort they spend.

For unpaid contributors, the return is not salary. It is a combination of:

1. **real ownership** of work that exists;
2. **documented proof** of what they built;
3. **skills and experience** they can explain in interviews;
4. **access to a wider student network** across Vizag;
5. **recognition and credentials** earned through completion;
6. **progression** into larger responsibility;
7. **community and relationships** that are enjoyable and meaningful; and
8. **performance-based tangible benefits**, including Clstr merchandise and eligible event passes.

If Clstr does not provide these returns, a person should not be expected to remain. The correct offer is not “work for our dream.” It is:

> **Take ownership of a defined project, build something real, receive proof of the work, grow your network and earn progression through performance.**

---

## 1. The three participation levels

| Level | What the person does | Commitment | What the person receives |
|---|---|---:|---|
| **Regular Member** | Participates in Circles, Guilds, events and collaborations. | No fixed commitment. | Belonging, cross-college discovery, activities, connections, event access and the ability to participate without a title. |
| **Campus Ambassador / Campus Captain** | Activates Clstr in one college during a defined 90-day sprint. | Approximately 2–3 hours per week, as agreed before selection. | Training, a real campus project, contribution record, certificate, recommendation-letter eligibility, performance rewards and progression consideration. |
| **Core Team Member** | Owns one department or defined operating function. | Approximately 4–6 hours per week during the agreed trial or event cycle. | Functional ownership, portfolio proof, leadership experience, cross-college network, feedback, certificate, recommendation-letter eligibility and consideration for larger responsibility. |

These levels are not status labels. They represent different levels of responsibility and return.

---

## 2. Why a Campus Ambassador should join

A Campus Ambassador should join if they want to become the person who connects their college to a wider student city. The role is useful for a student who wants practical leadership but cannot yet commit to owning a full department.

### What the Ambassador receives immediately

On acceptance, Clstr must provide:

- a written role description;
- the 90-day period and expected weekly time;
- a named Clstr manager;
- a campus activation plan;
- the reporting format;
- the code of conduct and safety rules;
- the milestone and reward rules; and
- a clear statement that the role is unpaid.

The Ambassador should not have to discover the conditions after joining.

### What the Ambassador builds

During the 90-day sprint, the Ambassador builds evidence of:

- student communication;
- campus-level outreach;
- event activation;
- community-building;
- reporting and accountability;
- feedback collection;
- coordination with other students; and
- responsible representation of a community.

That is more valuable than a title only if the Ambassador can show what they actually did and what changed because of their work.

### Campus Ambassador milestone ladder

The following are **internal campaign targets**, not verified facts about every college. Clstr must confirm them before each campaign and count only verified students, active clubs or spaces, meaningful participation and honest reporting.

| Milestone | Internal target | Benefit |
|---|---|---|
| **M01** | 500 verified students and 2 active clubs or community spaces. | Clstr completion certificate. |
| **M02** | 1,000 verified students and 4 active clubs or community spaces. | Role-specific letter of recommendation, provided when the documented work supports a truthful letter. |
| **M03** | 1,500 verified students and agreed domain clubs or spaces active. | Clstr merchandise, such as a cap or hoodie, subject to stock and size. |
| **M04** | 2,000 verified students and full activation of the agreed campus plan. | Clstr tee, free VIP passes to eligible Clstr events and recognition, subject to capacity and published rules. |
| **M05** | Top semester performer, assessed on quality, retention, reporting, conduct and community outcome—not raw signups alone. | Fast-track review for Campus Node Coordinator, Core Team consideration or a funded paid opportunity if one exists; appreciation letter and case-study feature with consent. |

The reward order is:

> **Certificate → recommendation letter → merchandise → tee/VIP passes/recognition → progression or paid-opportunity consideration.**

These are benefits, not salary. Fake accounts, spam, purchased signups, unsafe activity or unauthorised college representation do not qualify.

### Why should the Ambassador stay for 90 days?

The first weeks provide onboarding and a defined task. The middle period provides proof of execution. The final period provides a documented result, certificate, recommendation-letter eligibility, reward assessment and progression decision.

The Ambassador stays because the role has a visible finish line and because each completed stage produces something they can keep: a project, a record, a credential, a relationship, a reward or a next opportunity.

## 3. Why a Core Team member should join

A Core Team member should join if they want to own a real function in an emerging student community. They should not join for a decorative title or vague exposure.

### The five departments

| Department | What the pair owns |
|---|---|
| **Logistics** | Events, venues, run-of-show, day-of execution and vendors. |
| **Design + Social** | Content, brand, Instagram, event creatives and campus-specific handles. |
| **Outreach** | Sponsors, companies, colleges and ambassador coordination. |
| **Tech + Web** | `vizag.clstr.in` upkeep, form fixes, CollabHub support and basic troubleshooting. |
| **Finance + Ticketing** | Event budgets, ticket tracking, sponsor payments and surplus reporting. |

Every department has two people, and both must be able to operate it independently. The Founder owns vision, major relationships, hiring and high-risk approvals. The department pair owns normal execution.

### What the Core Team member receives

The Core Team member receives:

- a defined department and named responsibilities;
- authority to make routine decisions inside agreed boundaries;
- one real deliverable in the first 30 days;
- access to the tools and information needed to do the work;
- monthly feedback and blocker removal;
- cross-college operating experience;
- a portfolio case study based on real work;
- a contribution record;
- a completion certificate after the agreed period and deliverables are completed;
- a role-specific recommendation letter when documented contribution supports a truthful letter;
- public recognition with consent; and
- consideration for larger responsibility based on evidence.

### Why should the Core Team member stay?

They stay when the role keeps producing three things:

1. **Ownership:** they make decisions and see their work shipped.
2. **Evidence:** they can show outcomes, not merely attendance.
3. **Growth:** they receive harder problems, feedback and a credible next step.

A Core Team member should leave if the Founder keeps all decisions, work is repeatedly cancelled, feedback never arrives or the title is being used to obtain unpaid labour without providing ownership.

## 4. What holds people to the community

Retention is not created by one certificate at the end. It is created by a repeated operating rhythm.

### The Clstr retention loop

| Frequency | Clstr must provide | Contributor experiences |
|---|---|---|
| **Every week** | One clear priority, a named owner, a short check-in and blocker support. | Progress, accountability and help. |
| **Every two weeks** | Feedback on quality and a decision about what to continue, change or stop. | Learning and visible improvement. |
| **Every month** | A shipped community outcome, recognition of contribution and a review of personal goals. | Proof that their time produced something. |
| **At milestone** | Accurate assessment and the published reward. | Certificate, recommendation letter, merchandise, passes or progression when earned. |
| **At term end** | Contribution summary, exit or renewal conversation and next-step decision. | Closure, evidence and respect for their time. |

The strongest retention mechanism is not pressure. It is **progress that the contributor can see**.

## 5. The value Clstr provides beyond certificates

Certificates and recommendation letters matter, but they are not enough by themselves. Students can receive certificates from many organisations. Clstr must provide a stronger combination.

### 5.1 Real work

A Campus Ambassador should activate a real college project. A Core Team member should own a real operational output. “Attended meetings” is not a portfolio.

### 5.2 Proof of capability

Clstr should maintain a contribution record showing the role, dates, responsibility, work delivered, outcome, feedback and conduct. The contributor should be able to convert it into a portfolio case study.

### 5.3 Credible recommendation

A recommendation letter must state what the person actually did. It should not contain inflated claims. The person earns the letter through completed and documented contribution.

### 5.4 Access and relationships

Clstr gives students a reason to meet people beyond their own college: other organisers, creators, Guild members, Circle members, partners and student leaders. Access must never be presented as a guaranteed job or internship.

### 5.5 Recognition and identity

A contributor should be visibly associated with work they completed. Recognition can include event credits, a public spotlight with consent, a leadership badge, a case-study feature and Clstr merchandise.

### 5.6 Progression

A reliable Campus Ambassador can be considered for Campus Node Coordinator. A strong Coordinator or contributor can be considered for Core Team responsibility. A funded paid project can be offered only when it actually exists and has written terms.

## 6. What will not retain people

Clstr should stop using the following as its main pitch:

| Weak offer | Why it fails |
|---|---|
| “Join because the vision is huge.” | Future ambition does not pay for present time. |
| “You will get exposure.” | Exposure is meaningless without a defined audience, activity and outcome. |
| “You will meet important people.” | Access cannot be guaranteed and should not be exaggerated. |
| “You may get a job later.” | Speculation is not compensation. |
| “Add as many students as possible.” | Raw signups encourage spam and do not prove community value. |
| “You will receive a certificate” with no criteria. | The credential loses credibility. |
| “You are part of the core team” with no authority. | The title becomes misleading. |
| “Work until we become successful.” | Indefinite unpaid labour is unfair and causes attrition. |

The replacement is simple: **defined work, defined time, defined support, defined proof, defined reward and defined exit.**

---

## 7. The minimum promise Clstr must fulfil

Before recruiting people, Clstr must be able to fulfil this promise:

> **You will receive a defined role, a named owner, a real first project, regular feedback, a contribution record, a completion certificate when you meet the standard, recommendation-letter eligibility when your work supports a truthful letter, transparent performance rewards and a fixed-term exit or progression decision.**

If Clstr cannot fulfil this promise, it should recruit only ordinary members until the operating system is ready.

---

## 8. The direct answer to “Why should I stay?”

A strong answer is:

> **Stay if you are getting real ownership, real learning, real relationships and real proof from the work. During the 90-day cycle, Clstr will give you a defined project, a named person to support you, documented contribution, a certificate after completion, recommendation-letter eligibility based on completed work, performance-based Clstr rewards and a clear review for the next role. You are not staying for a vague promise of money or a title. You are staying because your work is visible, useful and building something that students across Vizag can use.**

That answer is credible only when Clstr actually delivers it.

---

## Final standard

> **Do not ask contributors to sacrifice for Clstr’s future. Give them a valuable present: ownership, learning, proof, recognition, relationships, rewards and a fair next step.**

The community will retain people when contributors can honestly say:

> “I joined for the opportunity, I stayed because I was trusted with real work, and I can now prove what I built.”

**Scope note:** This document concerns the Clstr Vizag community. `clstr.in` is the supporting technology layer for identity, discovery, registration, continuity and contribution records. It is not presented here as a separate product promise.
