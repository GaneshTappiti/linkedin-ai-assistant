## Slide 1

# Vizag Has Campuses. Students Need a City.

Every college has its own clubs, friend groups, events and opportunities. But student life should not end at the campus boundary.

Clstr exists to help students across Vizag:

- Meet people beyond their own college.
- Find friends, collaborators and communities.
- Participate in culture, sports, creators, careers, competitions and more.
- Feel that the whole city belongs to them.

> **Your college gets you a degree. Clstr gets you a city.**

|---|---|
| Coding community | Developers and technology |

## Slide 3

# The Community Is the Product

Clstr is not an app pretending to be a community. It is a living student community supported by technology.

Students come for:

- **Belonging:** friends, Circles, hangouts and experiences.
- **Building:** Guilds, collaborators, projects and creative teams.
- **Growth:** mentors, opportunities, recognition and access.

Events bring students together in the real world. The community keeps them connected after the event.

## Slide 4

# clstr.in Makes the Difference Real

Anyone can start a WhatsApp group. Clstr is building a persistent student network.

**clstr.in helps the community:**

- Give students a persistent identity across events and semesters.
- Help students discover relevant people, Circles, Guilds and events.
- Connect online participation to real-world attendance and relationships.
- Preserve community history even when student leaders graduate.

> **Clstr is the community. clstr.in is the layer that makes the community different.**

The app does not replace human connection. It helps human connection continue.

## Slide 6

# We Are Building From Every Campus

Clstr will grow through people who understand their own college and care about the student experience.

Campus ambassadors are not just promoters. They are community nodes who:

- Bring students into Clstr through trusted peer relationships.
- Share what students actually want, not what we assume they want.
- Build participation across Circles, Guilds and events.
- Help Clstr survive semesters, graduations and changing student leaders.

The goal is not to collect ambassador names. The goal is to build active, diverse and reliable campus nodes.

## Slide 7

# The Core Team Owns the Machine

We are looking for builders who want responsibility, not only a title.

| Department | What the two department owners will own |
|---|---|
| **Logistics** | Events, venues, run-of-show, safety, check-in and vendor coordination. |
| **Design + Social** | Brand, creatives, Instagram, student stories, social distribution and creator formats. |
| **Outreach** | Colleges, Campus Ambassadors, sponsors, companies, venues and community partnerships. |
| **Tech + Web** | clstr.in journey, forms, analytics requests, reliability, privacy escalation and basic support. |
| **Finance + Ticketing** | Event budgets, ticket sales, sponsor payment records, refunds and surplus reporting. |

There are **five departments with two people in each department**. Both people must be able to run the department independently; there is no permanent “lead and assistant” arrangement. Community/Collab is a cross-department operating responsibility, not a sixth department. Ownership must still be written and clear.

## Slide 8

# What You Get — And What We Expect

Clstr is currently an unpaid volunteer leadership opportunity. We will not hide that. The value is not a vague promise of “exposure”; it is defined ownership, real work, community access and documented development.

**You get:**

- Ownership of a real function or campus node—not just a title.
- A portfolio of shipped work: events, campaigns, community programs, partnerships or campus activations.
- Practical experience in leadership, communication, event operations, content, partnerships and team coordination.
- Access to students, creators, organisers, mentors and collaborators across Vizag.
- Structured onboarding, role playbooks, feedback and contribution records.
- A completion certificate after the defined role period and required deliverables are completed.
- A role-specific letter of recommendation when the work is successfully completed and documented well enough for a truthful letter.
- Honest recognition and consideration for higher responsibility based on delivered work.
- Eligibility for future paid tasks, reimbursements or rewards only when they actually exist and have written terms.

**We expect:** consistent contribution, honest reporting, student-first decisions, respectful collaboration and ownership of outcomes.

**We do not promise:** instant money, guaranteed fame, jobs, internships, equity or future payment. We do provide a completion certificate after the defined role period and deliverables are completed, and a role-specific recommendation letter when the contribution is successfully completed and documented. Any compensation must be formally structured and earned through a sustainable community business.

Choose pilot campuses, define active Circles and Guilds, repair the core clstr.in journey, and prepare the first community experience.

Our first proof is not a large crowd. It is students returning, connecting across colleges, and choosing to build the next experience with us.
