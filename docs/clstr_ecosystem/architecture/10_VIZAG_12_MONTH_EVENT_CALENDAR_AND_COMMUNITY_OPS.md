# 10 — Vizag 12-Month Event Calendar & Community Operations

> **The definitive operational blueprint for running Clstr Visakhapatnam across all 12 months — synthesized from `Vizag Community Playbook and Event Plans Overview` (v3 Reconciled).**

---

## 1. Operational Philosophy & Reconciled Doctrine

This operational framework reflects the **v3 Reconciled Master Playbook**. It rectifies the mistakes of early drafts:
1. **The Trust Doctrine (No Per-Lead Selling)**: Sponsors and brand partners pay exclusively for brand visibility, stage time, and room presence. Clstr **never sells student contact lists or phone numbers**, preserving absolute community trust.
2. **Lean, Zero-Debt Execution**: Operates without assuming venture funding. Flagships are structured to be cash-flow positive or break-even via low-cost ticket sales and local business sponsorships.
3. **Mandatory Venue Rotation**: While internal ambassador founding nights may occur at any campus, **public events must rotate venues across the city** (Dr. L. Bullayya in City Center, AU campus, GVP, public beach third spaces). Clstr must never be perceived as "a single college's project."
4. **The Weekly Living Layer**: A community cannot survive on 12 disconnected monthly spikes. A living weekly cadence maintains daily engagement between flagship events.

### Detailed Breakdown of Months 1 to 4 (The Critical Beachhead Phase)

#### Month 1: Founding Night (Ambassador & Core Launch)
* **Objective**: Bond the initial ambassador cohort and record the first 8 authentic student video testimonials.
* **Attendance**: Capped at 60–80 invited student leaders.
* **Format**: Dinner briefing, platform walkthrough, distribution of Ambassador kits, and unveiling of the 12-month roadmap.
* **Budget**: Minimal seed budget covered by founder.

#### Month 2: Campus Takeover #1 (The First Public Event)
* **Objective**: First open-doors gathering proving Clstr exists outside the digital screen.
* **Attendance**: 150–200 verified students from at least 8 colleges.
* **Format**: Casual student mixer, live acoustic set, no heavy corporate speeches.
* **Crucial Rule**: Venue rotates away from the Month 1 location to ensure it is visibly recognized as an independent city-wide community.
* **Timing**: Sunday, 1:30 PM to 5:30 PM (respecting all hostel curfews).

#### Month 3: Guild Mixer #1 (Collab Matching)
* **Objective**: Convert passive social attendees into active builders.
* **Attendance**: 100–120 students; capped to induce scarcity.
* **Format**: 45 minutes of structured skill-tag speed matching (each attendee wears a badge indicating *“My Guild”*, *“What I Build”*, *“Who I Need”*), followed by open project formation.

#### Month 4: City Explore & Action Day
* **Objective**: Deep cross-college bonding through non-academic activities.
* **Format**: Morning beach cleanup + mixed-college box cricket and food crawl near RK Beach / Tenneti Park.
* **Why It Works**: Zero tech intimidation; highest natural friendship formation across different college batches.

## 5. Ground Risk Register & Hard Mitigations

| Risk Identified | Probability | Impact | Hard Operational Mitigation Protocol |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Hostel Curfew Penalties** | High | Fatal | **Mandatory 1:30 PM – 5:30 PM Sunday schedule**. No event ever runs past 6:00 PM. Verified transit pools escorted by Captains. |
| **Perception as Single-College Project** | Medium | High | **Mandatory Venue Rotation**. Never host two consecutive public events at the same venue. Equal stage time for all campuses. |
| **Bureaucratic Campus Gatekeeping** | High | Medium | **Operate as City Entity**. Host events in neutral city auditoriums (Bullayya, A-Hub, cafes) bypassing college administration politics. |
| **Between-Event Energy Fade** | High | High | **The Weekly Cadence**. Enforce rotating Guild micro-prompts and Circle vibe-checks to maintain daily habituation. |
| **Financial Overextension** | Medium | High | **Zero-Debt Execution**. Free venues (incubation centers, beaches, partner cafes) and early-bird ticket pre-sales cover event hard costs. |
