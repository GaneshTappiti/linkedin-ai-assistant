## 1. Identity & Core Positioning

### 1.1. Strategic Mottos
* **Platform Motto**: *“Your college gets you a degree. Clstr gets you a city.”*
* **Community Motto**: *“Find your people. Vizag is the campus.”*

### 1.2. The Core Problem in Visakhapatnam
Visakhapatnam (Vizag) is Andhra Pradesh's premier industrial and technological metropolis, home to over **200,000+ college students** across 30+ engineering, degree, and professional colleges. Despite this massive density, student life is severely isolated:
* Students are trapped in geographical silos: Madhurawada in the north, Duvvada/Gajuwaka in the south, Tagarapuvalasa/Dakamarri on the outskirts, and Siripuram/Resapuvanipalem in the city center.
* Inter-college interaction is virtually zero outside of brief 2-day annual fests governed by inter-college rivalry.
* Talented builders cannot find teammates: a developer at Raghu has no way of finding a UI designer at Bullayya or a marketing co-founder at GITAM.

Clstr is Visakhapatnam's first verified cross-college student network, built on two equal pillars: **Collaborate** (building, projects, careers) and **Socialize** (belonging, culture, shared city life).

## 5. Operational Logistics: Curfews, Permissions, & Safety

Running an authentic student community in an Indian tier-2 city requires mastering real ground constraints:

### 5.1. The Hostel Curfew Reality
* **The Constraint**: Over 65% of female students in Visakhapatnam live in college or private hostels with strict evening curfews:
  * VIEW (Vignan Women's): **5:30 PM** curfew
  * GVPCE / AU Girls Hostels: **6:30 PM – 7:00 PM** curfew
  * Private Hostels (Madhurawada / Siripuram): **7:30 PM** curfew
* **The Solution**:
  * All flagship community events are scheduled on **Saturday or Sunday afternoons between 1:30 PM and 5:30 PM**.
  * No core programming runs past 6:00 PM, guaranteeing that every hosteler can return safely before curfew gates lock.
  * Verified Transit Pools: Campus Captains coordinate shared autos and RTC buses from central venues (Bullayya, Siripuram) back to peripheral campus clusters.

### 5.2. Campus Permissions & Administration Strategy
* Clstr operates as an **independent city-wide student platform**, not a college club. This bypasses bureaucratic delays and internal campus politics.
* Events are hosted at neutral, central venues:
  * Dr. L. Bullayya College Auditorium (City Center)
  * A-Hub / Andhra University Incubation Center
  * Neutral third spaces: public beach parks, local cafes, and private co-working hubs.

## 6. Campus Captains (Ambassador Network)

The physical growth engine of Clstr is its **Campus Captains**:
* **Team Structure**: 2 to 4 Captains recruited in every one of the 19 beachhead colleges (1 Tech Captain, 1 Social/Creative Captain).
* **Core Responsibilities**:
  * Distribute event posters and QR stickers across campus food stalls, xerox shops, and canteen noticeboards.
  * Seed official fest registrations and CollabHub team requests in college batch groups.
  * Staff the offline gate check-in stations during flagship summits using the Ticket-Platform scanner.
* **Incentive Ladder**:
  * Exclusive Captain swag (hoodies, badges).
  * Direct access to senior industry mentors and founders.
  * Verified Letters of Recommendation (LORs) and official Leadership Certificates issued on the platform.

## 7. Revenue & Sponsorship Strategy in Vizag

Clstr Vizag generates revenue across multiple local channels:
1. **Event Ticketing**: Nominal fees (₹99 – ₹299) for flagship hackathons, conferences, and fests managed through `Ticket-Platform`.
2. **Local Brand Sponsors**:
   * Food & Beverage: Cafe chains (Bean Board, Ironhill, F3 Cafe) sponsor hackathon snacks in exchange for student footfall.
   * Student Housing & PG Portals: Brand placements for local private hostels.
   * Education & Coaching Hubs: GATE/GRE/Study Abroad consultancies sponsoring career panels.
3. **Company Talent Access**: Local IT companies (Tech Mahindra, Fluentgrid, Miracle Software Systems, Wipro) paying for access to verified top performers from the Hackathon Guild.
