# Ganesh's Real Conversation Tone — Reference for Automation

## Source: WhatsApp convo with Vishal Velidi (Apr 4-8, 2026)

## Key Patterns

### Opener style
- Casual, peer-to-peer, uses "bro" naturally
- Direct, short messages (1-2 lines max)
- Asks honest, blunt questions: "Is this something cool or another shit?"

### How he pitches
- Never sends a wall of text upfront
- Builds context first, asks questions, THEN explains
- Transparent about early stage: "right now we're not in a position to pay"
- Frames value as ownership: "not just helping, actually take ownership"
- Role is tailored to the person: "the best fit for you would be handling marketing and outreach"

### How he handles objections / delays
- Zero pressure: "That makes complete sense bro"
- Gives space: "Take your time"
- Offers to share updates meanwhile
- "I'd rather have someone who commits properly than jumps in casually"

### How he closes
- Suggests a sit-down call: "we can sit once and break down everything properly"
- Keeps door open without being pushy

## Tone Rules for Automation
1. MAX 2 short sentences per message
2. Use "bro" or first name, never formal addressing
3. Reference something SPECIFIC from their profile (not generic praise)
4. Ask ONE real question (not rhetorical)
5. No corporate language — sound like a 21yo founder, not a recruiter
6. No emojis overload, keep it clean
7. Be honest and direct, even blunt
