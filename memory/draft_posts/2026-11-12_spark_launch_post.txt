Every college event in India runs on the exact same broken stack.

A Google Form. A screenshot of a UPI transaction. An organizer standing at the gate scrolling an Excel sheet with 500 rows while 200 angry students wait in the heat.

It doesn't work.

Then, two weeks after the fest, your team spends 8 hours on a Canva mail-merge just to email certificates one by one.

We hated watching this happen on our campus.

Your club deserves better than a Google Form.

That's why we're building Spark (spark.clstr.in).

We designed it around three basic things: direct UPI payments with 0% platform cuts, sub-400ms QR passes scanned from any phone browser, and one-click bulk certificate generation.

Right now, Spark is in beta testing.

We're testing it live with a few student clubs in Vizag before opening it up everywhere. Zero platform fees. No payment gateway cuts.
