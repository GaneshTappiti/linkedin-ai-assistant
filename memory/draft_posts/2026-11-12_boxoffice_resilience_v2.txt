500 students at a campus fest entrance will break your ticketing app.

Happens every time.

At our last event, the college Wi-Fi died within 10 minutes. 350 people stood in line outside the hall, mobile data dropped to zero bars, and 40 students pulled out ₹349 in paper cash asking to pay right there.

Most ticketing tools assume everyone's got 5G and working UPI.

On an Indian engineering campus, that assumption kills your line before you've even opened.

So when building the event ticketing layer for our campus community, we had to rethink gate flow from scratch.

Instead of blocking walk-ins, we built an offline box-office mode directly into our TanStack Start app.

Here's how we did it:
Volunteers issue walk-in tickets in under 6 seconds.
The device generates an HMAC-SHA256 signed QR pass completely offline.
Our scanner validates the pass instantly using local crypto tokens without pinging the cloud.
Once the college network wakes back up, the ledger batches all 80+ transactions back to Supabase.

Zero gate delays. Zero stranded attendees.

When you're shipping software for physical spaces, you learn fast: the bottleneck isn't database latency. It's the 30-meter crowd waiting outside your door.
