WHAT A CLUB GETS
THREE JOBS NOBODY WANTS TO DO. DONE.

01 / SIGN-UPS

A shareable event page with your poster, price and details. Paid event? Attendees pay you directly on your own UPI, you approve them in one tap.

02 / THE GATE

Every approved attendee gets a ticket code and QR. Your volunteers check people in from a phone, and you watch the count live.

03 / CERTIFICATES

Upload your template, click where the name goes, hit generate. 600 personalised certificates, no spreadsheet. Files wipe themselves after 15 days.

WE NEVER TOUCH YOUR MONEY.

Spark does not collect, hold or process payments. For a paid event you put your own UPI ID or payment link on the page. Attendees pay you. You see it land in your app, and you approve the registration.

No gateway fees. No settlement wait. No cut for us. Ever.
